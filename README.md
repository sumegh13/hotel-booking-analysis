# Hotel Booking Demand Analysis - Tableau Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyzes hotel booking demand using Tableau Public to understand booking patterns, cancellations, seasonality, revenue trends, and guest origins. The goal is to identify business insights that can help hotels improve revenue forecasting, reduce cancellations, optimize pricing, and better target customer segments.

## Why This Project?

Hotel booking demand is affected by multiple factors such as fluctuating market demand, cancellation behavior, seasonal travel patterns, and customer origin. This project uses data visualization to convert raw booking data into clear business insights and actionable recommendations.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset includes:

- 119,390 hotel bookings
- 2 hotel types: City Hotel and Resort Hotel
- Booking records from 2015 to 2017
- 32 fields covering booking details, cancellations, guests, revenue, market segments, and customer geography

## Tableau Dashboards

The Tableau analysis includes two main dashboards:

### Dashboard 1: Hotel Booking & Cancellation Overview

This dashboard focuses on overall booking performance, cancellation trends, hotel type comparison, and key booking behavior patterns.

### Dashboard 2: Seasonality, Revenue & Guest Origin

This dashboard analyzes seasonal demand, revenue patterns, guest origin countries, and customer segments to support business forecasting and marketing decisions.

## Key Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations were identified:

- Redesign cancellation policies to reduce revenue loss
- Increase direct bookings to reduce dependency on third-party booking channels
- Optimize pricing dynamically based on seasonality and demand
- Improve room assignment algorithms to enhance guest satisfaction
- Target families for resort hotel packages
- Market to top-origin countries to improve customer acquisition
